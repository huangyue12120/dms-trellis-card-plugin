# v0.5 UI/UX Design Gate

## Goal

Record the approved v0.5 baseline before production QML implementation and
leave no blocking choice about modes, discovery trust, persistence, or scope.

## Requirements

- Review all three v0.5 design documents.
- Record accepted choices, rejected alternatives, known limitations, and the
  user's explicit approval date/statements.
- Map production child tasks to the approved state/component contracts.
- Keep rich v0.6/v0.7 and P2 surfaces out of the approval.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The Gate record names all approved modes and the default.
- [ ] Trusted-root, depth, remembering, revalidation, and read-only behavior are approved.
- [ ] Rejected unsafe/over-designed alternatives are recorded.
- [ ] Pill/popout and discovery implementation tasks can cite the frozen docs.
